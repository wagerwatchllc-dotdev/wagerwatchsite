Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Wager Watch LLC handles information when you use the Wager Watch mobile app, website, or contact our team.

Information we collectHow we use itHow we share itYour choicesContact

1. Who we are

Wager Watch LLC ("Wager Watch," "we," "us," or "our") develops tools that help adults understand and change their gambling habits. We are responsible for the personal information described in this policy.

2. Information we collect

Information you provide

  • Account and profile information, including your email address, username, sign-in credentials, and selected in-app avatar.
  • Onboarding and plan information, including your birth year, gender selection, past attempts to change your gambling habits, gambling frequency and amounts, currency, goals, limits, reasons for change, alternatives, concerns, and plan steps.
  • Activity you choose to record, including bet amounts and outcomes, check-ins, urges, triggers, emotions, notes, commitments, and progress.
  • Watcher conversations, including messages you send and responses you receive when you use the optional AI companion.
  • Communications, such as information included in support requests or feedback.

Information collected through the service

  • Service information, such as authentication, feature-usage, and basic request records used to operate and secure the service.
  • Device and notification information, including device platform, time zone, notification preferences, and an Expo push token when your device permits push notifications. A token may be refreshed while device permission remains available, even if you turn off alerts in Wager Watch.
  • Purchase information, including subscription product and entitlement, trial or paid status, purchase and expiration dates, renewal status, and a store transaction identifier. Payment credentials are processed by the app store and are not provided to us.
  • Website requests, such as technical request information received by our website host when you visit wagerwatch.dev.

3. How we use information

We use information to provide and personalize Wager Watch; save and display your plan and progress; generate Watcher responses when requested; provide in-app reminders and, with device permission, push alerts; confirm subscription access; respond to support requests; maintain safety and security; and troubleshoot or improve the service.

Wager Watch does not provide betting picks, odds analysis, or gambling strategy. We do not sell personal information.

4. How we share information

We share information as needed to operate the service, comply with law, protect rights and safety, or complete a business transaction. Providers used by the current app and website include:

  • Supabase for accounts, authentication, app data, and server functions;
  • Apple for App Store distribution, purchases, and device notifications;
  • RevenueCat for subscription management and entitlement status;
  • Expo Push Service to deliver push alerts when enabled;
  • OpenAI when you send a Watcher message; and
  • GitHub Pages to host this website.

When you use Watcher, the app sends OpenAI your message and relevant context to generate a reply. That context can include your username, birth year and gender selection, plan, recent bet amounts, outcomes, triggers and notes, urges, emotional check-ins, commitments, recent Watcher messages, and time zone. We also send a pseudonymous identifier derived from your account ID for safety purposes. We store Watcher messages and responses in your Wager Watch account.

These providers process information for the services they perform for us and are subject to their own contractual and legal obligations. We may also share information with professional advisers or authorities when required by law.

5. Retention and security

We keep account and activity information while your account is active and for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, satisfy legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. In-app account deletion requests removal of your Wager Watch account and associated app records and deletion of the linked RevenueCat customer record. If deletion fails, your account remains available and you can contact us for help. App Store subscriptions and billing records are managed separately by Apple; deleting your Wager Watch account does not cancel a subscription.

Support correspondence and limited records may be retained when needed for legal or operational reasons. We use administrative and technical safeguards designed to protect information, but no method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

6. Your choices and rights

You can change push-alert settings in Wager Watch and notification permission in your device settings, clear your Watcher chat history in the app, and delete your account from Settings. Depending on where you live, you may also have rights to request access, correction, deletion, or a copy of your personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing.

To make a privacy request or get help with deletion, email support@wagerwatch.dev. We may need to verify your identity before completing a request.

7. Age limits

Wager Watch is intended for adults and is not directed to children. Do not use the service if you are under 18 or under the age of majority where you live.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the service develops or legal requirements change. We will post the revised policy here and update the effective date above.
