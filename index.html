The product

Support for the moments that shape progress.

Changing a gambling habit is rarely one decision. Wager Watch brings planning, reflection, and in-the-moment tools into one focused experience.

01
◎

Build a personal plan

Choose whether to stop or reduce gambling, set practical targets, and keep your reasons for change visible.

02
↗

See honest progress

Record activity, check in with how you feel, and understand patterns without turning one difficult day into failure.

03
⏸

Interrupt an urge

Create space between an impulse and an action with guided pauses, urge logging, and a safer next step.

04
◉

Return to the plan

Watcher, an optional AI support companion, uses your plan context to help you reflect and refocus after a hard moment.

Our approach

Practical support. Human language. No judgment.

01

Start with awareness

Clear records help make triggers, emotions, and spending patterns easier to recognize.

02

Make the next action smaller

A pause, a check-in, or a call to someone you trust can change the direction of a difficult moment.

03

Keep progress honest

Wager Watch is designed to encourage accountability without shame, including after setbacks.

+

Important support

You do not have to handle a crisis alone.

Wager Watch is a self-management and educational tool, not medical care, therapy, diagnosis, or emergency support. If gambling is putting you or someone else in immediate danger, contact local emergency services. In the United States, call or text 1-800-MY-RESET for the National Problem Gambling Helpline.

The company

Wager Watch LLC

Wager Watch LLC develops digital tools for adults who want to build more awareness and control around gambling behavior. Our first product is the Wager Watch mobile app, currently in development for iPhone.

We believe behavior-change technology should be direct, respectful, and transparent about what it can—and cannot—do.
