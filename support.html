Common questions

Quick answers

Having trouble with the app?

Close and reopen Wager Watch, then make sure you have an internet connection and the latest app version. If the problem continues, email us with your device model, app version, and what you were trying to do.

Need help with your account?

For sign-in or account access problems, email us from the address you use for Wager Watch. We may ask you to verify that the account belongs to you before making changes.

Manage a subscription

On iPhone, you can view or cancel an App Store subscription in Apple Subscriptions. Wager Watch also links to subscription management from Settings. Deleting your Wager Watch account does not cancel store billing.

Delete your account or request your data

To delete your account in the app, open Settings, choose Delete Account, and follow the confirmation. For privacy questions or help with a data request, email support@wagerwatch.dev. Read our Privacy Policy for more information.

Need immediate help?

Wager Watch is a self-management tool and cannot provide emergency or crisis support. If you or someone else is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services. For help with gambling, reach out to a qualified professional or a local gambling support service.
